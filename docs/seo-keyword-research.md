# Squeegeez — Keyword Research & SEO Map

**Business:** Squeegeez Window & Exterior Care
**Location:** Castlegar, BC (serving the West Kootenays)
**Primary services:** Residential + commercial window cleaning, window track & screen cleaning, gutter cleaning, pressure washing

> **On the numbers below:** volume and difficulty are *directional estimates* based on
> market size (a small BC town + surrounding towns), not live tool data. For exact
> figures, plug these into Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account),
> Ahrefs, or SEMrush. For a market this small, most **local** terms are **low
> difficulty and low-but-high-intent volume** — the whole game is Google Business
> Profile + a handful of well-optimized local pages, not chasing big volume.

## 1. Competitors

Local service SEO has three competitor tiers. Run these searches from a Castlegar
location (or with `&near=Castlegar,BC`) to get the **live** list — don't assume:

- `window cleaning Castlegar`
- `window cleaners near me` (from a Kootenay location)
- `gutter cleaning Nelson BC`
- `pressure washing Trail BC`

**Tier 1 — Direct local competitors** (other window/exterior cleaners in the Kootenays).
These are who you fight in the organic results and the Google Local Pack. Record each
one's: Google Business Profile review count + rating, whether they have a real website,
and which service pages they rank for.

**Tier 2 — Adjacent trades** that also bid on these terms: general pressure-washing
companies, handyman/property-maintenance businesses, eavestrough/roofing outfits.

**Tier 3 — Directories & aggregators** that outrank small businesses organically:
HomeStars, Yelp, Bark, Jobber's "find a pro" pages, Google's own Local Services.
You usually can't beat these organically — the play is to **be listed on them** and to
win the **Local Pack** (which sits above them) via Google Business Profile.

**Deliverable to fill in:** a 5-row table — Competitor | GBP rating (#reviews) | Website? | Ranks for | Gap we can exploit.

## 5. Search-intent analysis of the ranked results

For "`window cleaning [town]`" queries in a market like this, the SERP is almost always:

1. **Local Pack (map + 3 businesses)** — driven by Google Business Profile proximity,
   reviews, and categories. *This is the top prize and it's not something on-page text wins.*
2. **Directories** (HomeStars/Yelp/Bark) in the organic slots.
3. **A few local business websites** — service-area landing pages with town names,
   services, photos, and reviews.

**Implications for Squeegeez:**
- Intent is **commercial/transactional-local** → pages should lead with the service +
  town + a clear "Get an Estimate" CTA (already the case). Don't turn service pages into blog posts.
- **Google Business Profile is priority #1.** Reviews are the biggest lever in the Local Pack.
- Real before/after photos and reviews on-page match what ranking competitors show.

## 6. Questions people ask (target with an FAQ + FAQ schema)

Pulled from typical "People Also Ask" for these services. Answer these in an FAQ section
(home or a dedicated `/faq`) and mark it up with `FAQPage` schema for rich results:

- How much does window cleaning cost? / What's your pricing?
- Do you clean windows inside and outside?
- How often should I get my windows cleaned?
- Do you clean screens and tracks too?
- How often should gutters/eavestroughs be cleaned in BC?
- Are you insured / WorkSafe BC covered? *(Yes — already a selling point on the site.)*
- What areas do you serve?
- Do you do commercial / recurring contracts?
- Do you pressure wash driveways and house siding?
- Can you clean windows in winter?
